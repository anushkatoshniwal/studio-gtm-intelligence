# GTM Intelligence Agent + Experiment Lab

An AI-powered GTM workflow that turns customer, product, and market intelligence into testable opportunities and experiment decisions.

## What it does

The workflow connects two pieces:

**GTM Intelligence Agent**
- Searches across customer, product, and market intelligence
- Identifies the strongest converging GTM opportunity
- Defines the target segment and hypothesis
- Provides supporting evidence, risks, and working experiment assumptions

↓

**Experiment Lab**
- Imports the opportunity and assumptions
- Lets the operator edit the assumptions
- Models the potential economic impact
- Evaluates the experiment
- Produces a GO / NO-GO / REFINE decision

## Why I built this

I wanted to explore how AI agents could move beyond summarizing intelligence and actually help a GTM team turn signals into structured experiments and decisions.

This is a demo/prototype built to explore that workflow.
